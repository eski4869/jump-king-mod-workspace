# Human Verification Checklist

- Test every map in the current Progression List where practical.
- Test official content and at least one custom map.
- Test SaveStates, Jump%, Player Coordinates, MoreSaves, and other major mods.
- Confirm Player Behaviour Modifier detection is appropriate.
- Confirm gameplay graphics, overlay positions, and ending screens.
- Confirm custom-block behavior for additional players.
- Confirm settings and output files remain compatible and understandable.
- Confirm normal shutdown, title return, map switching, and forced process exit.
- Confirm no stale components, duplicated menu entries, or repeated HTTP servers.
- Confirm Debug logging is disabled in the published Release build.
